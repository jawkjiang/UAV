\subsection{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

\subsubsection{Model architectures}

Table~\ref{tab:architectures} summarises the seven detector architectures.
All models receive the same input shape $(L, F) = (50, 9)$ and output a
single sigmoid probability.
No batch normalisation layers are used; dropout ($p=0.3$) is applied to
intermediate representations in LSTM, BiLSTM, GRU, and Transformer models.

\begin{table}[!t]
\caption{Detector architecture summary.}
\label{tab:architectures}
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lllr}
\toprule
\textbf{Model} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Key parameters} & \textbf{Param.}\\
\midrule
CNN         & 1-D convolution   & 3 conv layers, 64/128/256 filters       & $\sim$200K \\
LSTM        & Recurrent         & 2 layers, 128 hidden units              & $\sim$200K \\
BiLSTM      & Recurrent (bi-dir)& 2 layers, 64 hidden units per direction & $\sim$200K \\
GRU         & Recurrent (gated) & 2 layers, 128 hidden units              & $\sim$130K \\
CNN-LSTM    & Hybrid            & 2 conv + 1 LSTM (64 units)              & $\sim$160K \\
TCN         & Temporal conv.    & 8 blocks, dilation $2^i$, 64 filters    & $\sim$170K \\
Transformer & Self-attention    & 2 layers, 4 heads, $d_{model}=64$       & $\sim$130K \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsubsection{Training hyperparameters}

All models share the same training configuration:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Optimiser: Adam, learning rate $10^{-3}$, default betas
  \item Loss: binary cross-entropy (BCELoss; models output sigmoid probabilities)
  \item Batch size: 64
  \item Maximum epochs: 100; early stopping patience: 10 (monitored on validation BCELoss)
  \item Random seeds: $\{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}$ (5 independent runs per model)
  \item Hardware: AMD Radeon 8060S iGPU with 62 GB HBM3E unified memory; ROCm 7.2
\end{itemize}
